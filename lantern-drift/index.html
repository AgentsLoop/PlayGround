🏮 Lantern Drift

Rooftop Courier. Guide your paper lantern across the sleeping city. Collect signal sparks ✦, slip through sweeping searchlights, and reach the safehouse 🏮 at the far rooftops.

Hold Space/↑/W or press & hold to rise — release to sink. Nudge with ←→ / AD. On touch, drag toward where you want to go. P pause · R restart · M mute.
