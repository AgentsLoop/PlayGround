NEON PARCEL PANIC

Steer with WASD / arrows. Pick up a glowing parcel, deliver it to the drop zone of the same color. Avoid red patrol drones. You have 90 seconds and 3 lives. Each delivery: +100 and +5s.

R = restart  •  P = pause  •  M = mute

How to play: Drive over a parcel to pick it up (one at a time). Then drive into the matching colored drop pad. Wrong pad = no delivery. Drone hit = lose a life, drop parcel, 2s shield. Survive the clock!
