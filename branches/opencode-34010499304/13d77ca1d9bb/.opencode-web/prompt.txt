Build a small playable browser game called "Orbit Orchard". The player controls a tiny astronaut gardener who moves around a circular planetoid and collects 8 glowing fruit before a 60-second timer expires. Use keyboard controls plus pointer/touch-friendly controls, show a clear HUD with fruit count, timer, and restart button, and provide satisfying collection feedback and a win/timeout state. Keep the presentation polished, readable, and playable in a desktop browser.
