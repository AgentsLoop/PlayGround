HOW TO PLAY

GOAL

Destroy all 5 rival vehicles before they destroy you. Your hull regenerates only on the glowing green repair pad (center-north). Grab floating pickups to stock missiles & mines.

WEAPONS

🔥 MG — unlimited, spread + recoil. Hold Space / LMB.
🎯 Homing (1) — locks nearest enemy ahead.
🔥 Fire (2) — fast straight rockets, burn.

✦ SPECIAL (G) — unique per vehicle, cooldown 20s.

TIPS
