How to Play

Objective

Move the glowing cup to catch blue raindrops and avoid red sparks.

  • Blue drop = +10 points
  • Red spark caught = −1 life
  • Missed blue drop = −1 life

Controls

◀ ▶ or A D to move
Hold for smooth glide • P pause • Touch & drag on mobile

Difficulty

Every 100 points you level up: rain falls faster and spawns more sparks. How long can you glow?

Neon Field Notes

Visual Language

Dark city rain, cyan rim-light, pink hazards. Blue drops leave a cool comet tail, red sparks crackle and fizz.

Pro Tips

  • Stay centered — you can reach both edges faster.
  • Red sparks fall slightly faster. Watch their orange core.
  • Don't chase every blue at low lives; survival >贪心.

Accessibility

All actions work with keyboard. No mouse required. Pause anytime with P or Esc.
