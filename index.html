How to play (controls, rounds, defense)

Goal: drain the enemy health bar. Best of 3 — first to 2 round wins takes the match. 60-second timer; timeout goes to the healthier fighter.

P1: AD move · W jump · hold S block · F slash (fast) · G smash (heavy) · H spirit bolt (cooldown) · P pause · M mute.

P2 local: ←→ move · ↑ jump · hold ↓ block · J slash · K smash · L bolt.

Defense: blocking negates 80% damage and prevents knockdown, but chip damage still leaks. Jump over bolts. Heavy attacks break crouch distance but are slower.

FINISH HIM: on match point, when the loser hits 0 HP you get a 3-second window — step close and land a smash for a dramatic finisher.

Accessibility & recovery

High-contrast HUD (gold on black), scaled text, ARIA live announcements of rounds, timer warnings and results, full keyboard operability (arrow keys work in fighter select), touch buttons ≥52px driving P1 (2P local needs a keyboard), prefers-reduced-motion freezes shake, particles, torch flicker, floor scroll and idle bob, pause on tab-hide in every combat phase, and one-key rematch. If audio fails, the game continues silently.
