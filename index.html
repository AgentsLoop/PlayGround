Field Manual

Goal

Raze every Crimson Hive structure (Core, Gates, Sentinels, Pylons, Extractors). Lose all of your own and you fall.

Economy

Collectors carry 5 alloy per trip from blue crystals, 4 plasma from green vents (needs Extractor). Right-click a crystal to gather; new Collectors can be rallied with right-click while the Core is selected. Keep training workers; expand to the side fields when yours run dry.

Building

Select a Collector → build buttons (or B pylon, G gate, E extractor, T sentinel, C core). Green ghost = valid. Collector walks over and constructs it.

Army

Lancers (fast, cheap), Bulwarks (tanky, gas-hungry). Drag-select, A-click to attack-move, focus Sentinels with right-click. Watch the minimap for red pings.

Vision

Unexplored = black. Explored = dim. Only attack what your units currently see — scouting wins games.
