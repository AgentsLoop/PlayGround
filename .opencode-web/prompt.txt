# Mortal Combat\n\n## Objective\n\nDeliver Mortal Combat as a polished, immediately playable browser fighting game for players who want fast, readable one-on-one combat. Treat Mortal Combat as the fidelity target: preserve the recognizable arcade fighting-game visual language, round flow, combat readability, dramatic presentation, and responsive interaction model while creating original implementation and presentation assets.\n\n## Quality bar\n\n- Research authoritative and high-signal references before coding. For the visual direction, inspect real screenshots, gameplay footage, and interface views; select at least six anchors, record their URLs and purpose, and use them to guide the work.\n- Deliver the real web game surface with a playable fighter-versus-fighter loop, distinct combatants, attacks, blocking or defense, hit feedback, health and round state, win/loss flow, restart behavior, responsive controls, and clear audio-visual feedback.\n- Inspect the actual running artifact with vision and direct browser interaction. Compare captures against the selected references and review the arena, fighters, HUD, menus, effects, responsive layout, and recovery states side by side or with blind A/B review where practical.\n- Meet a production-quality bar: coherent art direction, legible combat at a glance, stable input handling, accessible controls and contrast, complete requested states, graceful loading and failure behavior, and delivery evidence for the working web build.\n\n## Execution\n\n- Have the lead choose the route and divide the project into independently judgeable pieces.\n- Give every important piece a builder and a fresh-context critic or verifier. Require the verifier to inspect the actual artifact, compare it with the quality bar, identify the largest remaining gap, and send the work back for correction.\n- Sustain the build, verify, and improve loop across as many sessions as necessary.\n\n## Verification\n\n- Fix the largest remaining gap and repeat the build/inspect/critic cycle until the acceptance bar is met, or stop only with explicit evidence of the unmet criteria and blockers.\n- Record concrete evidence for combat behavior, visual quality, responsive interaction, accessibility, recovery behavior, and delivery.\n\n## Progress\n\nMaintain a canonical live progress page that another agent can resume without losing context. Keep references, decisions, completed work, evidence, blockers, current gaps, and the next exact action up to date.
