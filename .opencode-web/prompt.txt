# twisted metal

## Objective

Deliver a small production-quality, browser-based recreation of **Twisted Metal** gameplay for desktop web with playable vehicle combat, level progression, and score feedback.

## Quality bar

- Research authoritative references and collect real references before coding.
- Download at least six high-signal visual/reference assets or screenshots into a local `reference-assets` directory and record source URLs beside each local filename.
- Inspect the actual artifact with real browser interaction tools and compare behavior against the source references.
- Preserve core input, combat, camera, and pacing mechanics that are visible in the requested source.
- Provide recovery and failure states, restart/resume flow, and clear game-over and win conditions.

## Execution

- Split the work into judgeable pieces and assign each piece a builder and a fresh-context verifier.
- Build iteratively across sessions until the acceptance bar is met, prioritizing gameplay responsiveness and polish.
- Keep implementation and verification evidence tied to the requested visual and mechanical targets.

## Verification

- Compare the current result with each acceptance point from the quality bar and fix the largest remaining gap first.
- Record concrete evidence of behavior, performance, and UX quality after each improvement loop.

## Progress

Maintain a resumable live progress page with references, evidence, decisions, blockers, gaps, and next actions.
