# twisted metal\n\n## Objective\n\nDeliver a production-quality browser game inspired by Twisted Metal for players who want fast, chaotic vehicular combat. Preserve the recognizable visual language, interaction model, combat loop, arenas, vehicles, weapons, destruction, and real web-playable surface of the reference while creating original implementation and presentation.\n\n## Quality bar\n\n- Research authoritative references, screenshots, footage, and interface views before coding; select at least six high-signal visual and interaction anchors and record each URL and purpose.\n- Build complete playable workflows with real vehicle control, aiming, shooting, pickups, enemy encounters, damage and destruction states, arena navigation, restart/recovery, responsive input, audio/feedback, accessibility or operability, and a shippable deployed web artifact.\n- Inspect the actual running artifact directly and compare captures with the acceptance bar side by side or by blind A/B review.\n- After every completed visual attempt, use a fresh-context critic who writes no code and acts as a brutal AAA art director. Require fresh screenshots from several viewpoints and zoom levels, inspect the running artifact, and score design and aesthetics from 0 to 10. Treat above 8.5 as AAA quality, 7 as good indie quality, and 5 as programmer art. Pass only with at least 28.5 total across agreed criteria and zero blocking errors; otherwise return ranked issues to the builder for another attempt.\n\n## Execution\n\n- Act as the CTO of the game studio and orchestrator for continuous multi-day work. Own the product and technical vision, roadmap, delegation, quality, risks, decisions, integration, and delivery.\n- Choose the route and divide the project into independently judgeable pieces. Assign a separate builder and fresh-context critic or verifier to every important piece.\n- Have each verifier inspect the actual artifact, compare it with the bar, identify the largest remaining gap, and send the work back for iteration.\n- Continue delegating, integrating, implementing, verifying, and improving across as many sessions as needed. Treat prototypes, demos, and smoke tests as intermediate milestones.\n\n## Verification\n\n- Repeat the build/verify/improve loop until the acceptance bar is met or the run stops with explicit evidence.\n- Do not accept any visual piece below the 28.5 critic threshold or with blocking errors; rank defects and retry.\n- Record concrete evidence for behavior, quality, and deployment, including research links, screenshots, test results, and recovery behavior.\n\n## Progress\n\nMaintain a canonical live progress page that another agent can resume. Record references, decisions, completed work, evidence, blockers, current gaps, and the next exact action.
