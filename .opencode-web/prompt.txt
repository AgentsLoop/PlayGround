# add hermes support

## Objective

Deliver production-quality Hermes support in the project for its users. Preserve the existing product behavior while making Hermes a complete, usable supported option.

## Quality bar

- Research authoritative Hermes documentation, relevant integration examples, and executable acceptance fixtures before coding.
- Implement the complete requested workflow, including normal operation, configuration, errors, recovery behavior, and operability.
- Inspect the actual artifact with relevant tools and compare it directly with the acceptance criteria.

## Execution

- Have the lead choose the implementation route and split the work into independently judgeable pieces.
- Give every important piece a builder and a fresh-context critic or verifier.
- Continue across as many sessions as necessary; do not stop after an initial implementation or smoke test.

## Verification

- Verify the supported Hermes workflows using concrete evidence and record delivery evidence.
- Identify the largest remaining gap, fix it, and repeat until the acceptance bar is met or the run is explicitly stopped with evidence.

## Progress

Maintain a resumable live progress page with references, decisions, completed work, evidence, blockers, gaps, and the next exact action.
