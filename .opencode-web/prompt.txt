## Objective

Deliver a production-quality 3D browser first-person shooter for desktop players, using Call of Duty: Warzone as the fidelity reference for its recognizable military visual language, responsive combat feel, HUD, loadout flow, and match loop. Ship original code and original or properly licensed assets; do not use proprietary game files.

## Quality bar

- Research before coding: inspect official Call of Duty materials, representative gameplay footage, screenshots, and interface views. Select at least six high-signal references, record each URL and its purpose in the live progress page, and use them to guide the visual and interaction decisions.
- Preserve the real web 3D surface: deliver a playable browser game with first-person camera and movement, aiming, shooting, reload/ammo, health or armor, enemy combat, hit feedback, a clear start state, a combat objective, win/lose state, restart flow, and responsive desktop controls. Do not substitute a flat mockup, static scene, or screenshot.
- Inspect the actual running artifact directly in the browser and compare captures or side-by-side views against the reference bar. Verify the game at the public URL, including the important combat and recovery states.
- Meet a production-quality bar for visual hierarchy, readable HUD, lighting, feedback, performance, accessibility or operability, error handling, and complete delivery evidence.

## Execution

- Act as the CTO of the game studio and the orchestrator for continuous multi-day work. Own the product and technical vision, roadmap, delegation, quality bar, risks, decisions, integration, and delivery. Keep working ambitiously toward the complete outcome; treat prototypes, demos, and smoke tests as intermediate milestones.
- Choose the implementation route and divide the project into independently judgeable pieces. For every important piece, use a builder and a fresh-context critic or verifier. The verifier must inspect the real artifact, compare it with the quality bar, identify the largest remaining gap, and send the work back for improvement.
- Delegate, integrate, implement, verify, and iterate across as many sessions as needed. Keep proprietary references informational only and make the shipped result original or properly licensed.

## Verification

- Continue the build/verify/improve loop until the acceptance bar is met or an explicit blocker genuinely requires user input. Fix the largest remaining gap after each inspection; do not stop after the first build or smoke test.
- Record concrete evidence for research, gameplay behavior, visual quality, browser inspection, public deployment, and delivery. Verify start, combat, damage, reload, win/lose, restart, and failure-recovery paths.

## Progress

Maintain a canonical resumable live progress page with reference URLs and purposes, decisions, completed work, evidence, blockers, current gaps, and the next exact action so another agent can continue without losing context.
