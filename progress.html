1. Quality bar — Twisted Metal Black (PS2)

Every builder/critic round is judged against this bar. Blind compare: does the current index.html beat it?

  • Dark industrial arena: grimy asylum / factory / rooftop, fog, flickering lights, destructibles.
  • Chase cam: tight third-person follow, shake on hits, FOV kick on boost.
  • Armed cars: machine guns + missiles / specials per vehicle, lock-on or aimed fire.
  • Pickups: health, weapons, turbo scattered in arena, respawn timers.
  • HUD: health, weapon/ammo, radar/minimap, timer, kill feed.
  • AI aggression: 3+ bots that hunt, flee when weak, grab pickups, ram.
